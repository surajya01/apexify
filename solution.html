Technical FAQ

Do you inherit and modernize legacy codebases?

Yes. We often execute "Strangler Fig" migration patterns—wrapping legacy monoliths in modern API gateways and gradually decoupling domains into scalable microservices without downtime.

How do you ensure data security?

We implement zero-trust networking, RBAC (Role-Based Access Control), AES-256 encryption at rest, and TLS 1.3 in transit. Our pipelines include automated SAST/DAST vulnerability scanning.

What is your typical technology stack?

While we are tech-agnostic, we prefer TypeScript/React (Next.js) for the frontend, Node.js or Golang for backend compute, and PostgreSQL for relational data, orchestrated on AWS or GCP.
